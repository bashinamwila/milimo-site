Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

Milimo is a WhatsApp service run by Applied Intelligence Ltd that helps people register businesses with the Patents and Companies Registration Agency (PACRA). This policy explains what information we collect when you use Milimo, why we need it, who we share it with, and what you can ask us to do with it. We handle personal data in line with Zambia's Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021.

Who we are

Applied Intelligence Ltd, Lusaka, Zambia.
Email: bashinamwila@gmail.com
WhatsApp: +260 771 198 435

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number, the profile name you have set on WhatsApp, and the messages, photos and documents you send us.
  • Details of your business: the names you propose, what the business does, and its address.
  • The personal details PACRA requires for the people in the business (owners, directors, shareholders and secretaries), such as full names, NRC or passport numbers, nationality, addresses, contact details and photos of identity documents.
  • Payment confirmations, such as mobile money transaction references and amounts. We never ask for your mobile money PIN and cannot see your balance.
  • The letters, certificates and status updates PACRA sends about your application.

We ask for your consent before we collect personal details for an application. If you give us details about other people, such as your co-directors, please make sure they agree to it.

How we use it

  • To prepare your applications and submit them to PACRA, and to any other government agency you ask us to deal with.
  • To send you updates, results and documents on WhatsApp.
  • To keep the records the law requires, and to answer questions or complaints about our service.

Milimo uses software, including AI tools, to read your messages and fill in forms. Nothing is submitted to PACRA until you have seen a summary of it and approved it.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • PACRA and other government agencies, only for the applications you ask us to make.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), which carries our messages. WhatsApp's privacy policy covers how it handles your use of WhatsApp.
  • Mobile money providers, when you pay a fee.
  • Service providers that host our systems or provide software we use, including AI services. They may only use your information to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, where the law requires us to.

Some of these providers, including Meta, may store or process information outside Zambia. When that happens we only use providers that are bound to protect it.

How long we keep it

  • Photos of identity documents are deleted once the PACRA filing they were needed for is complete.
  • Records of applications and payments are kept for as long as Zambian law requires.
  • Other messages are deleted when you ask us to, unless we still need them for an application in progress or the law requires us to keep them.

How we protect it

Only the people at Applied Intelligence Ltd who need your information to do the work can see it. Information travels over encrypted connections, and identity documents are stored encrypted.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act you can ask us to:

  • show you the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your information;
  • stop using it, or withdraw your consent.

Withdrawing consent doesn't undo an application already submitted to PACRA. Once a business is registered, PACRA keeps its own records, and we cannot remove them.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Zambia.

Deleting your data

Send "Delete my data" to Milimo on WhatsApp, or email bashinamwila@gmail.com from an address we can match to you. We will confirm your request and delete your information within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep.

Children

Milimo is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information you have already given us, we will tell you on WhatsApp.
