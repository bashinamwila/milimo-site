Milimo

Business registration help on WhatsApp, from Applied Intelligence Ltd

Milimo helps small business owners in Zambia register with PACRA without visiting an office or using the PACRA website. You chat with Milimo on WhatsApp. She checks whether your business name is available, collects the details PACRA needs, and keeps you posted until your certificate is ready.

What Milimo can do today

  • Name clearance: check whether the name you want can be registered
  • Company incorporation
  • Business name registration

PACRA's fees are paid by you directly to PACRA. Milimo never holds them.
